Frequently Asked Questions

Will my QR code ever stop working or expire?

No. Standard static QR codes store your direct payload (URL, text, or Wi-Fi data) permanently in the pixel matrix. Since there is no redirect server involved, the QR code never expires, has no scan limits, and requires no subscription.

Why do other websites charge subscriptions for QR codes?

Commercial websites create "dynamic" QR codes that route scans through their proprietary servers. Once you print the codes on physical menus or flyers, they deactivate the redirect after a 14-day trial and demand $15–$40/month to keep the links active.

Can I print these QR codes on merchandise and menus?

Yes. Select the 1024 × 1024 px (Print / 4K) option and Level H Error Correction for maximum sharpness and scan reliability on physical media, packaging, signs, and brochures.
